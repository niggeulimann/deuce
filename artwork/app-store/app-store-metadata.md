# English (U.S.)

## App Name

deuce - Tennis Tracker

## Subtitle

Simple scoring & match stats

## Promotional Text

Stay focused on your match: track points and serving position on Apple Watch,
then explore your results and stats on iPhone.

## Description

More tennis. Less mental math.

Deuce guides you through every match right from your wrist. Record points with
just a few taps, keep the score, server, and serving position in view, and
focus on what really matters: your game.

After the match, your results sync to the Deuce companion app on iPhone. Review
past matches, assign opponents, and discover how your game is developing.

ON APPLE WATCH

- Record points quickly and easily
- See the current score at a glance
- Keep track of the server and correct serving position
- Choose traditional scoring or No-Ad
- Play one set, best of three, or best of five
- Select four or six games per set
- Save the court surface for every match
- Review match history directly on your watch
- Optionally record a tennis workout in Apple Health

ON IPHONE

- Browse and open your match history
- Add opponents and compare head-to-head records
- See wins, losses, win rate, and your current streak
- Follow your win-rate trend over time
- Compare results across court surfaces
- Discover average match duration and longest rallies
- Add details and notes to individual matches

Deuce is designed to feel fast, friendly, and uncomplicated. There is no
overloaded dashboard during play, no distraction between points, and no
account to set up before you can begin.

Workout tracking is optional. When enabled, Deuce asks for permission to read
relevant activity data and save your tennis workout to Apple Health.

Apple Watch is required for live match tracking.

## Review Notes

Deuce consists of an iPhone companion app and an Apple Watch app. No account,
registration, subscription, or demo credentials are required.

Suggested review flow:

1. Open Deuce on Apple Watch.
2. Choose the first server, number of sets, games per set, court surface, and
   optional No-Ad scoring.
3. Tap Play and record several points. The score, server, and serving position
   update during the match.
4. Complete or exit the match and open History on Apple Watch.
5. Open Deuce on the paired iPhone. Match data is transferred using
   WatchConnectivity. If it has not arrived yet, use Sync iPhone in the Watch
   app while both apps are available.
6. On iPhone, review Matches, Opponents, and Stats. Opponents and notes can be
   added from a match's detail view.

HealthKit access is optional. Enable Track Workout on the Watch start screen
to test it. If permission is granted, Deuce reads relevant workout metrics and
saves the tennis workout to Apple Health. All scoring and companion features
remain usable without granting Health access.

## App Privacy Draft

Based on the current implementation:

- No user account is required.
- No advertising or third-party analytics SDK is present.
- Match information is stored on the user's devices.
- Match information is transferred between the paired Apple Watch and iPhone
  using WatchConnectivity.
- HealthKit access is optional and used for tennis workout tracking.
- Health data is not used for advertising or tracking.

If no match or HealthKit data is transmitted to the developer or a third-party
server, select "Data Not Collected" in the App Privacy questionnaire. Confirm
this again before submission if analytics, crash reporting, cloud sync, or
other network services are added.
